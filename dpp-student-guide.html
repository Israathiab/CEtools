1 · Why this exists

The EU's Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR, Reg. 2024/1781) and the Batteries Regulation (Reg. 2023/1542) introduce the Digital Product Passport: a machine-readable record attached to a product that travels with it from production to end-of-life. By the late 2020s, almost every physical product placed on the EU market — from batteries and electronics to textiles, furniture and white goods — will need one.

For large multinationals this is an expensive compliance project. For an SME exporting to Europe, or supplying parts and materials into someone else's EU-bound product, it can be existential — without a DPP, the customs door closes.

DPP Compass is a single screen that pulls together the four things an SME actually needs to do:

  • Account for waste in mass and dollars, so the business case for change is visible.
  • Find recycled-input alternatives in the supply chain and quantify the saving.
  • Track products in the field via QR codes so end-of-life material can come home.
  • Generate the DPP record itself — every field, with status and verification.

2 · The two parts of the system

DPP Compass has two interfaces:

InterfaceUsed byWhat it does
Manufacturer dashboardYou — the SMEWaste, supply chain, BOM/BOP, tracking, recovery, DPP compliance, redesign opportunities.
Loop consumer appYour customerScans the QR on the product, sees the DPP, locates take-back points, earns rewards for returning the product at end-of-life.

The two are tightly coupled. Every QR scan in the app lights up in your dashboard. Every take-back drop-off feeds your recovered-material ledger and your recycled-content claim.

3 · Three entity modes

The dashboard works for three kinds of company. Use the toggle in the top right.

ModeWho it's forWhat changes
End-product manufacturerYou sell finished products to consumers (white goods, furniture, lighting, electronics).Full QR consumer tracking, take-back network view, consumer-facing DPP.
Parts supplierYou make motors, PCBs, frames, sub-assemblies for OEMs that ship to the EU.Batch-level DPP, B2B offcut return, your DPP feeds your customer's DPP.
Material supplierYou make polymers, alloys, fibres, pellets, billets sold to parts suppliers.Per-batch DPP with chain-of-custody, recycled content & carbon footprint as the headline claim.
Tip. A material supplier's DPP is upstream of everything. If you sell rPET to a parts maker who sells to an end-product brand, your batch DPP is what lets the brand claim recycled content on the consumer's QR scan.

4 · What's in each tab

Overview

Four KPIs (waste, savings, in-market units, DPP readiness) plus a 12-month trend and a live QR-scan feed. The donut shows what share of your material input is recycled.

Waste & Value

Production waste by material category and by line, in kilograms and USD. Each material row shows the current disposal path (landfill, energy recovery, re-melt, etc.) and the best-available path — with the dollar amount you'd recover by switching. "Hotspots" flag anywhere your scrap rate beats the sector benchmark or your waste cost crosses a threshold.

Supply Chain

Every material in your active BOMs is listed with virgin and recycled price per kg, recycled-content availability, lead time, and DPP traceability. A negative saving means recycled is currently more expensive — but you may still want it for the DPP claim. The supplier shortlist below filters to DPP-ready partners only.

BOM & BOP

Multi-level Bill of Materials and the production sequence (Bill of Processes) for the currently selected product. Each component shows its mass, cost, recycled %, and country of origin — the data that has to populate the DPP. Each process step shows energy use, scrap rate and embodied CO₂e.

Product Tracking

Where your products are in the world — based on consumer QR scans. The heat map is regional density; the stacked bar splits the installed base by lifecycle stage (early / mid / late / awaiting EoL / recovered).

Disposal & Recovery

What's coming back from the consumer app to your take-back partners — by stream, mass, value, and whether it closes the loop. Below that, the partner network shown in the consumer app, and the rewards consumers receive for each scan and drop-off.

DPP Compliance

The complete list of DPP fields required for this product group, with status (complete / partial / missing) and verifier. The DPP card on the right is a preview of what a consumer's QR scan will display. The SKU roster at the bottom is your project plan — every SKU you sell into the EU, and what's blocking its DPP.

Circular Opportunities

For the selected product, automatic flags across five lenses (disassembly, recyclability, life extension, material substitution, service model). Each opportunity comes with a per-unit savings estimate, a CO₂e estimate, and tags. The scorecard at the bottom is your product's circularity profile.

5 · How information flows

  1. You record your BOM and BOP per product — the master data.
  2. The system computes waste, cost, recycled-content and footprint per SKU from those records.
  3. It checks them against DPP required fields for the product group and tells you what's missing.
  4. It generates a QR code per unit, linked to the live DPP.
  5. The consumer app exposes the DPP, repair instructions, and take-back locations.
  6. Consumer scans (purchase, mid-life, EoL) feed back into the tracking tab.
  7. Take-back drop-offs feed the recovery tab, which feeds your next batch's recycled content — closing the loop.
Important. All numbers in this demo are illustrative. Real production data lives in your MRP/ERP. The point of the demo is the shape of the workflow, not the figures.

6 · A 60-second primer on the EU DPP

WhatDetail
Legal basisESPR (EU 2024/1781) for most product groups; EU 2023/1542 for batteries.
First product groupsBatteries (Feb 2027), then textiles, electronics, ICT, furniture, iron & steel, tyres, detergents.
FormatMachine-readable record linked from a data carrier on the product — usually a QR code or NFC tag.
Who must complyAny economic operator placing the product on the EU market. Non-EU exporters comply via their EU authorised representative.
Typical contentIdentifier, manufacturer, material composition, recycled content, substances of concern, carbon footprint, repair info, expected lifetime, end-of-life instructions.
VerificationMandatory fields must be sourced and verifiable. Third-party verification is required for some product groups and recommended for all.

7 · Where to start

  1. Pick your entity mode. Switch the toggle in the dashboard header to match what you sell.
  2. Pick one product. Don't try to fix all SKUs at once — pick the highest EU-revenue product first.
  3. Open the BOM & BOP tab. Make sure the structure matches your actual product. Every gap here becomes a DPP gap.
  4. Go to DPP Compliance. Look at the missing/partial rows. These are your concrete task list — one row, one task.
  5. Open Supply Chain. Identify two materials where recycled input is high-availability and either price-positive or close to parity. Those are your quick wins.
  6. Open Circular Opportunities. Pick the top opportunity by combined dollar + CO₂ impact and brief your design team.

Most SMEs can close the gap to a market-ready DPP in 4–6 months of focused work if they start now.
