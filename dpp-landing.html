The problem

Europe just changed the rules. Most SMEs aren't ready.

By the late 2020s, almost every physical product placed on the EU market will need a Digital Product Passport — a machine-readable record of materials, recycled content, carbon footprint, repair information and end-of-life routes. Enterprise compliance vendors charge six figures. SMEs need something else.

How it works

From your BOM to a passport, in one workspace.

DPP Compass ingests the product data you already keep, computes what the regulation asks for, and gives the consumer a QR code that closes the loop back to your shop floor.

01

Map the product

Upload or build the BOM and BOP. The platform calculates recycled content, carbon footprint, and waste cost from the data you give it.

02

Find better inputs

The supply-chain view ranks every material by the dollar saving and DPP impact of switching to verified recycled or low-carbon suppliers.

03

Generate the DPP

Every required ESPR field is mapped and status-tracked. When complete, a QR code is generated for every unit you ship.

04

Close the loop

The consumer app guides users to take-back points at end-of-life. Recovered materials feed your next batch and lift your recycled-content claim.

Who it's for

Built for every tier of the chain

Whether you make the finished product, the parts inside it, or the raw material it starts as — DPP Compass models your data and your obligations.

Tier 0

End-product manufacturer

You sell finished products to consumers across the EU — white goods, furniture, lighting, small electronics, textiles.

  • Consumer-facing QR + DPP
  • Installed-base tracking
  • Take-back network management
  • Per-SKU compliance roster
Tier 1

Parts supplier

You ship motors, PCBs, frames, sub-assemblies to OEMs that sell into Europe. Your batch DPP feeds theirs.

  • Batch-level DPP records
  • Customer offcut return tickets
  • Critical raw materials disclosure
  • OEM-ready data exports
Tier 2+

Material supplier

You make polymers, alloys, fibres, pellets, billets. Your DPP is the foundation of every claim downstream.

  • Per-batch chain-of-custody
  • Recycled content attestation
  • ISO 14067 carbon footprint
  • Premium pricing on verified DPP
